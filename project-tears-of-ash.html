Tears of Ash

Unreal Engine 5 | Horror

Overview

Tears of Ash is an atmospheric first-person horror experience created in Unreal Engine 5. It leverages the latest engine technologies to create a sense of dread through lighting and environmental detail.

Technical Focus

Developed over 3 weeks, the project focused on Lumen's dynamic global illumination to create realistic flickering light sources and high-fidelity shadow work. I also implemented a custom post-processing effect to simulate falling ash.

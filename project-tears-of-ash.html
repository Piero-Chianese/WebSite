Tears of Ash

Unreal Engine 5 | HorrorJam 2k24

Description

Tears of Ash is a game developed in Unreal Engine 5, created in one week for a horror-themed game jam.

Step into a chilling horror experience inspired by P.T., where memories, dreams, reality, and guilt blend into a twisted nightmare. Navigate through a haunting atmosphere as you confront disturbing visions and unravel the secrets buried in your mind. Each fragment of your past holds a clue, challenging you to connect the dots and uncover the dark truth lurking beneath the surface.

Will you face the thoughts that torment you, or will you succumb to the horrors within?

Angry Bean

Mobile Adventure | Pixel Art

Overview

Angry Bean is a charming mobile adventure game developed as part of a specialized game development path. The game tells the story of a "little dude" who embarks on a grand journey to help his family and fulfill his dream of becoming a master hero.

The core experience focuses on precise movement and climbing mechanics, challenging players to master the controls as they navigate through a beautifully crafted pixel-art world.

Innovative Mechanics

A unique feature planned for future development is the integration of emotion recognition using ARmotion. This would allow the game to adapt its mechanics based on the player's real-time emotional state, creating a more immersive and personalized experience.

Design & Art

As the Pixel Artist for this project, I focused on creating a vibrant and expressive world that complements the simple yet addictive gameplay. The visual style was designed to be both nostalgic and modern, ensuring clarity on mobile screens while maintaining a high level of detail.

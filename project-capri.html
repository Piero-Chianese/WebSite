Project Capri

Business Idea | AR & Gamification

The Concept: CAPRI

CAPRI (Come At Pub Relying on Imagination) was born as a pilot for a business idea aimed at solving the common issue of waiting times in pubs and restaurants. Our research showed that pub-goers are only willing to wait 8 minutes for a drink and 12 minutes for food, while the average wait per party is 23 minutes.

To improve this experience, we partnered with Ruin, a pub in Caserta, to create Unsolved Ruins—an app that transforms waiting time into an engaging adventure.

Try Unsolved Kitchen (Beta) Download Presentation

Core Experience

The app guides users through a path of alternating riddles, mini-games, and augmented reality (AR) scenes.

Riddles

Randomly presented challenges that encourage teamwork. Clues appear if the group gets stuck, ensuring a smooth flow of entertainment.

Mini-games

Built with SpriteKit, utilizing the iPhone's accelerometer and haptic feedback to create an immersive, tactile experience.

AR Scenes

Innovative use of AR to frame paintings or walls for clues. Features a custom light-management algorithm for dim pub environments.

Technical Innovation

We successfully integrated voice recognition for puzzle solving and video playback on 3D objects within AR scenes. These technologies allow for a highly scalable project that can be replicated in museums, art galleries, and other public venues.

Business Model

The value proposition focuses on helping entrepreneurs improve customer experience and retention. Revenue is generated through partnership fees and strategic in-app advertising, creating a sustainable ecosystem for both the venue and the platform.

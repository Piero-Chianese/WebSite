SusPack

Unity | Stealth Platformer

Overview

SusPack is a 2D stealth platformer where players navigate through a corporate office to recover sensitive data. The project focuses on AI detection mechanics and environmental puzzles.

Technical Focus

Developed in Unity over 3 weeks, I implemented a robust line-of-sight system for the guards and integrated physics-based platforming mechanics. The art style uses clean, stylized vector graphics.

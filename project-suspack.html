SusPack

Unity | Global Game Jam

About

In SusPack you'll play as an average businessman that mistakenly sent some nasty packages to some people while in a drunken state. Your objective is to infiltrate the warehouse where the packages are stored and regain them before sun rises.

The warehouse is protected by robot sentries and covered in bubble wrap, make sure not to be captured by them or you'll end up in jail and, worst of all, the packages will be sent to their destinations. Your only friend will be the bubblewrap-shooting machine you've found as you entered the warehouse. Observe, sneak through and don't make noise in order to be successful in your mission! But be careful, the time is ticking, the sun won't wait for you!

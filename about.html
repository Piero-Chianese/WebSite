About Me

Hello, my name is Piero, I am 30 years old and I hold a degree in Computer Science.

Since I was a child, I’ve always had a great passion for technology, especially computer science. This curiosity has driven me to explore the inner workings of software and hardware alike.

I also have a deep love for music — I’ve been playing guitar and piano for years, which I consider an essential outlet for creativity and expression.

Beyond technology and music, I’m a true motorsport enthusiast. I love everything related to cars, racing, and high performance. I’m passionate about sim racing and absolutely crazy about Formula 1. I also enjoy the sense of freedom that comes from riding my yellow motorcycle — for me, speed, precision, and control perfectly reflect the mindset I apply in both life and programming.

My Career

Foundation & Energy Drive

I obtained my computer expert diploma in 2014, earning several PON certifications and the ECDL. My first major role was at Energy Drive in Nola, a renewable energy company. I quickly moved from designing photovoltaic systems to managing IT systems, developing internal software, and handling social media presence.

IT & Medical Tech (Lefa)

While attending university, I worked at Lefa in Naples. I was responsible for repairing software for medical devices like ultrasound scanners and maintaining complex internal networks. This period allowed me to apply reverse engineering concepts to understand sophisticated medical equipment.

The Path to Game Dev

At the Apple Developer Academy, I discovered my true calling. Creating interactive prototypes and combining logic with creativity made me realize that game development is where I belong. Currently, I am refining these skills at Digital Bros Game Academy in Milan, focusing on Unity and Unreal Engine.

Human & Professional

“Two heads are better than one, and four are better than two.”

I work exceptionally well in teams, building strong professional relationships and valuing collaboration between creative minds.

I have strong abilities in problem finding, problem setting, and problem solving — often through creative and unconventional approaches. I am precise and reliable, but I also love pushing my limits.

One More Thing…

For me, creativity is the core of innovation. I always try to look at things from different perspectives, connecting ideas that may seem unrelated at first.

This mindset allows me to discover hidden opportunities and transform them into tangible results. My diverse experiences have shaped the way I approach design, development, and problem-solving.

Career Objectives

The future is full of possibilities, and I’m eager to explore them. My primary goal is to start my own company dedicated to developing VR experiences and video games.

I’m also deeply interested in augmented reality and automation technologies, which I believe will play a key role in shaping the future of digital experiences. Combining my passions for technology, creativity, and interactivity is the fuel for my journey.

GreenRoute

Unity | Management / Smart City

Overview

GreenRoute is a management game focusing on ecological urban planning. Players must design efficient routes for electric vehicles to minimize pollution and optimize city travel.

Design & Development

Developed in Unity over 1 month, the project features a top-down management interface with real-time traffic simulation. I implemented custom pathfinding algorithms to simulate green routing.

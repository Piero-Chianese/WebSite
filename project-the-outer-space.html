The Outer Space

SpriteKit | iOS Game

Overview

The Outer Space (TOS) is a mobile game designed for casual gamers looking for engaging entertainment in their free time. Developed in just 5 weeks, the project was a journey from zero knowledge of game development to a fully functional iOS game.

In TOS, you play as a rookie spaceship pilot of the Earth’s Royal Guard, sent into the unknown "Outer Space" to extract resources from distant planets and reinforce Earth's defenses against mysterious enemy armies.

Videos

Key Features

  • 4 Challenging Missions: Diverse objectives and environmental interactions.
  • 48 Spaceship Versions: Extensive customization for your primary vessel.
  • Resource Extraction: Strategic lander deployment on planets.
  • Dynamic Combat: Fight ordinary enemy ships and powerful bosses.
  • Customizable UI: Switch between free shoot and button modes; left/right stick placement.

Technical Implementation

One of the biggest challenges was translating ideas into a playable game with no prior experience. We utilized SpriteKit for core gameplay mechanics, UIKit and Storyboards for robust menu systems, and AVFoundation for custom-composed music and sound effects.

The design follows a colored, flat, and minimalist aesthetic to maintain clarity in the space environment. Every asset—from planets to asteroids—was hand-crafted to ensure a unique visual identity.

Beta Testing & Feedback

During the external TestFlight phase, we collected almost 350 sessions. User feedback was instrumental in refining the controls (introducing the shoot button option) and balancing the gameplay. We achieved a stable build with minimal crashes, proving the effectiveness of our technical architecture.

Business Model & Future

TOS adopts a Freemium model, offering the core experience for free while allowing users to purchase aesthetic customizations and components using both in-game currency and IAP systems.

Future updates will include a Multiplayer mode via Game Center, a component-mixing system for personalized ships, and a story-driven campaign to further expand the lore of the Earth’s Royal Guard.
